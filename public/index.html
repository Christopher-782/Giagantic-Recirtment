Who We Are

A Legacy of Excellence in Travel

Founded in 2008, Gigantic Tours And Guide Ltd has grown from a small boutique agency into one of the most trusted names in tourism. We specialize in curated travel experiences, corporate retreats, adventure tourism, and hospitality services across Africa, Europe, and the Americas.

Our team of 200+ passionate professionals works tirelessly to ensure every journey is seamless, memorable, and enriching. We're proud to be a brand that travelers trust and employees love.
